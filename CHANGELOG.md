# Changelog

## v1.2.0-alpha.1 — 2026-10-12

First public open-source snapshot, based on the existing V1.2 local workbench.

- Director/context/artifact workbench for product videos and creator content.
- Source intervals, original audio synchronization, local transcript/SRT workflows.
- Scoped proposals, image/video candidates, adoption and content-version recovery.
- Compatibility-first route suggestions, quotes, alternatives, exclusions and budget records.
- Two versioned internal skills with fixed allowed commands.
- Local render, review gates, MP4/SRT/ZIP and project restoration.
- Public-install packaging, explicit optional ASR model preparation, user/developer/privacy documentation.
- Create-project errors now stay visible inside the dialog, with input preserved for retry.
- Persistent connection notice and API/worker log files in the local data directory.
- Synthetic local smoke test; no paid provider or internal client media in the public distribution.

Alpha boundaries: Apple Silicon is the verified target; segment-level editing, single operator,
foreground service lifecycle, user-supplied provider accounts, and independent commercial-output review.
No native installer, general plugin marketplace, complete infinite canvas, multi-user service or SLA.
